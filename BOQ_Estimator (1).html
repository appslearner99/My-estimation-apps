What this tool does

This app auto-fills rate analysis for BOQ items using two source documents you provided:

  • Bhutan Schedule of Rates (Civil) - 2025 — base rates for individual Labour (LB), Material (MT) and Equipment/Machinery (MP) codes, for four base towns.
  • Labour and Materials Coefficients (Civil Works) - 2025 — how much of each labour/material/equipment code goes into one unit of a finished work item.

Type an item code, the app looks up its coefficients, multiplies each by the base rate for your selected town, groups the result into Labour / Material / Equipment / Sub-item cost, and adds the standard loading used in your BOQ workbook: +10% contractor profit, +5% tools & wastage, +1.5% water charges (matching the RATE ANALYSIS sheet in your BOQ_ESTIMATION.xlsx).

Some work items are themselves built from other composite items (e.g. a drain item that consumes an earthwork item and a plastering item) — the app resolves these recursively.

Coverage in this build

Chapters included: Earthwork, Drainage, Mortars, Concrete Works, Damp Proofing, Reinforced Concrete, Brick Work, Stone/Mud, Steel, Flooring, Roofing, Plastering, Painting and Wall Paper, Road Works, Clearing & Grubbing (support chapter). Chapters not included: Dismantling & Demolition, Wood Work, Tiling, Plumbing (indoor/outdoor), Additions/Alterations/Repairs, Conservation Works in Heritage Sites.

If a component code can't be found (rare OCR gaps in the source PDFs), the row is flagged in red in the breakdown and treated as zero — check it against the PDF and enter the rate manually if needed.
